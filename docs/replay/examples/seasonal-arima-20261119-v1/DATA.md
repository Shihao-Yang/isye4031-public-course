# Hotel room occupancy
Traveler’s Rest operates four hotels. The question is how many rooms will be occupied on an average day in each future month, to plan staffing, supplies and budgets. These 168 observations cover 14 consecutive years, beginning in January. t is the month index, month is 1 through 12, and rooms_per_day is the monthly average number of occupied rooms per day across the hotels. It is not a percentage or a monthly total. Calendar years and the total capacity are not specified.
Source: Bowerman, O’Connell and Koehler, Forecasting, Time Series, and Regression, fourth edition, Table 11.1, p. 491, Examples 11.1-11.3. The original numerical example uses the fourth-root scale before differencing. All 168 observations have appeared in prior course analyses. Historical splits are retrospective illustrations, not untouched tests. No observations for months 169-180 are supplied. No historical software fit has been newly executed here.
