# Teaching series
Bowerman, O’Connell and Koehler, Forecasting, Time Series, and Regression, fourth edition. Historical teaching examples; calendar dates unspecified.
- cod.csv: Table6.1 p282, 24 monthly cod catches in tons. These data appeared in the previous smoothing lesson.
- thermostat.csv: Table8.1 p359, 52 weekly thermostat sales in units.
- bikes_ch8.csv: Figure8.9 p370 quarterly bike counts (16); the Chapter8 version, distinct from Table6.8.
- tiger.csv: Table8.2 p377, 32 quarterly sports drink sales in thousands of cases.
- cola.csv: Table7.1 p329, 36 monthly cola sales in hundreds of cases.
- paper_towels.csv: Table9.1 p403, 120 weekly paper towel sales ABOVE 100000 rolls, in units of 10000 rolls. Actual rolls = 100000 + 10000*y. Negative y means below the baseline, not negative sales. No future observations beyond week120 are supplied. Previous course analyses already inspected all these historical data. Any chronological split is a retrospective illustration, not an untouched test.
